We're a software organization that aims to provide open-source software solutions to the community.
Our goal is to create innovative and user-friendly applications that can help people in their daily lives.

### Mission & Vision

We believe in the power of collaboration and open-source, and we are committed to building a community of developers who share our vision.

Our mission is to create innovative and user-friendly open-source software solutions for the community.

And our vision is to build a community of developers who share our vision and values, and to create software that can make a positive impact on people's lives.
